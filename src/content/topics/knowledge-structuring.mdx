Knowledge without structure is noise.
Structure without knowledge is empty form.

The interesting work happens at the boundary -- finding the right shape
for the things you know, so they become useful not just to you,
but to the systems and people around you.

## Schemas as shared language

When you define a schema for a type of knowledge, you are making
a commitment: this is what matters about this kind of thing.

That commitment creates a shared language.
